# User Guide

## ICONIC Digital Library and Repository System

Welcome to the **ICONIC Digital Library and Repository System** user guide. This guide explains how students, faculty researchers, lecturers, and guests navigate and use the system.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Browsing as a Guest
- Visit `/` or `/catalog` to browse open-access academic publications and books.
- Read abstracts, bibliographic details, citations, and consult public documents in the integrated PDF reader.

### 1.2 Account Registration
1. Navigate to `/register`.
2. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Institutional Email** (`@iconic-library.edu`), **Student/Staff ID**, and select your **Academic Department**.
3. Choose a password (at least 6 characters).
4. Newly registered users are assigned the **Student / Registered Member** role by default.

### 1.3 Signing In & Switching Roles for Evaluation
- Navigate to `/login`.
- Enter your credentials or click on the **Evaluation Quick Login** pills to preview the system as an **Administrator**, **Librarian**, **Lecturer**, or **Student**.

---

## 2. Main Navigation & Modules

### 2.1 Unified Search (`/search`)
- Use the search bar at the top of every page or on `/search`.
- Filter results by **Resource Type** (Books, Theses, Dissertations, Capstones, Journals, Reports).
- Refine by **Faculty/Department**, **Discipline Category**, and **Access Level Policy**.
- Switch between **Grid View** and **Table View** using the view buttons.

### 2.2 Digital Academic Library (`/library`)
- Dedicated catalogue for academic textbooks and monographs.
- Filter by discipline category and department.
- Click **Details** to see publisher, edition, ISBN, and generate APA or IEEE citations.
- Click **Read** to launch the integrated PDF viewer.

### 2.3 Research Repository (`/repository`)
- Scholarly archive for master's theses, doctoral dissertations, conference proceedings, and capstone projects.
- View academic supervisor attribution, departmental affiliation, and complete abstracts.

### 2.4 Document Reader (`/reader/[id]`)
- Integrated PDF viewer featuring:
  - Page navigation controls (Previous, Next, Page Jump).
  - Zoom controls (50% to 200%).
  - Rotation (90° increments).
  - Full-screen viewing toggle.
  - Collapsible side metadata panel.
  - Authorized **Download** button with signed URL security.

---

## 3. Personal Study Space

### 3.1 Bookmarks & Reading List (`/activity`)
- Click the bookmark icon on any book or research card to save it.
- View your **Currently Reading** materials and resume directly at your saved page.
- Review your **Saved for Later** study list.

### 3.2 Scholar Profile (`/profile`)
- Update your legal academic name, contact phone, and biographical summary.
- Update your account password.
